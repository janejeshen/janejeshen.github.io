## 1. Medical Appointment No-Show Analysis

### Overview

Analyzed more than **110,000 healthcare appointment records** to identify factors associated with missed appointments.

### What I Did

- Performed exploratory data analysis across demographic, scheduling, and appointment characteristics.
- Investigated attendance patterns across different patient groups.
- Applied **Mann-Whitney U** and **Chi-Square** statistical tests.
- Analyzed the relationship between waiting time and appointment attendance.
- Investigated attendance patterns across age groups.
- Translated findings into data-driven recommendations for scheduling and patient engagement.

### Key Finding

The analysis identified **waiting time as an important factor associated with no-shows**, with longer delays associated with higher missed-appointment rates.

### Tools

`Python` `Pandas` `NumPy` `Matplotlib` `Seaborn` `SciPy` `Jupyter`

## 4. Sentinel-2 Multi-Temporal Crop Classification

### Award

**2nd Place — Top Female**

### Overview

Developed a pixel-level machine learning model to classify **cocoa, rubber, and oil palm plantations** using multi-temporal Sentinel-2 satellite imagery.

### What I Did

- Processed geospatial raster datasets.
- Engineered spectral, vegetation, spatial, and temporal features.
- Built a LightGBM classification pipeline.
- Used Optuna for hyperparameter optimization.
- Applied GroupKFold cross-validation to reduce the risk of spatial leakage.
- Conducted extensive model experimentation and tuning.
- Evaluated model generalization across geographically distinct regions.

### Result

Achieved a **cross-validation Macro F1 score of 0.8663**.

### Tools

`Python` `LightGBM` `Optuna` `Scikit-learn` `Sentinel-2` `Geospatial Data` `Machine Learning`
